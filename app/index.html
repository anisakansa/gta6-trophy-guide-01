    How this guide is built

    Trophy names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags are the official ones, as published on PlayStation and listed by Exophase, cross-checked against PSNProfiles. The guides, the kind groupings and the roadmap stages are this guide’s own work, written for this entry — they draw on the official descriptions plus PowerPyx and GTABase, and they never copy another guide’s text. Where an official description is cryptic and no cited guide had confirmed the exact trigger at launch, the guide says so instead of inventing a step.

    Kinds (story, side missions, activities, collectibles, exploration, combat, heists, money, odd jobs) are our grouping, not PlayStation’s. Points are the PlayStation values: platinum 300, gold 90, silver 30, bronze 15 — 1,350 in total. Your checklist is stored only in this browser (localStorage); export saves it as a JSON file you can import on another machine. Nothing here needs the network: open index.html with the network off and it works.
